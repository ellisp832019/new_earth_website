# 07 Energy and Technology

## Current distortion

Technology is often shaped by surveillance, addiction, centralization, and profit-first design.

Energy systems are centralized and dependent on fossil fuels that poison Earth. Digital technology is weaponized for manipulation and data extraction. Attention is harvested as a commodity. Technology increases inequality by concentrating power in fewer hands. Devices are designed for planned obsolescence. Human agency and autonomy are eroded.

## New Earth design

Technology becomes a servant of life, freedom, learning, health, and ecological restoration.

Energy systems are decentralized, renewable, and respect Earth's limits. Technology is designed to preserve human dignity, privacy, and agency. What we build serves wisdom, connection, and life—not manipulation or profit extraction.

## Principles

- Technology must preserve human dignity and agency
- Energy systems must align with Earth's regenerative limits
- What we build should reduce harm and increase wisdom
- Technology should enhance real life, not replace it
- Privacy is a right, not a commodity
- Power should be distributed, not centralized

## Features

- **Decentralized renewable energy** — Solar, wind, geothermal, and other renewables distributed across communities rather than controlled by central monopolies
- **Energy storage and micro-grids** — Battery storage and local energy management systems increase resilience
- **Ethical AI** — AI systems are transparent, accountable, and designed to serve human flourishing, not concentrate power
- **Privacy-respecting communications** — Encrypted, decentralized platforms that protect privacy and reduce surveillance
- **Technology for restoration** — Innovation directed toward water purification, food production, health, housing, and ecosystem restoration
- **Open-source and transparent design** — Reducing dependency on corporate black boxes; community-governed technology where possible
- **Analog alternatives** — Technology is optional; alternatives for essential services exist for those who want to opt out

## Key questions for implementation

- How do we transition from fossil fuels to decentralized renewables at scale?
- What safeguards prevent AI from concentrating power?
- How do we create digital systems that can't be weaponized for manipulation?
- What's the role of technology in a flourishing civilization that isn't addicted to devices?
- How do we ensure technology serves healing and not just convenience?

## Connection to other pillars

- **Environment**: Renewable energy is essential for ecological restoration
- **Governance**: Transparent, distributed digital systems support participatory governance
- **Education**: Technology should enhance learning, not replace human connection and creativity
- **Health and healing**: Technology should support wellbeing, not create addiction and unhealthy dependence
