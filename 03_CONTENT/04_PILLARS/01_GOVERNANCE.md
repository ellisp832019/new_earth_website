# 01 Governance

## Current distortion

Power is concentrated, reactive, opaque, and often captured by wealth, fear, and division.

Leadership is often reviewed by charisma alone, and decisions are made behind closed doors. Accountability flows upward to those with power, not downward to those affected. Communities have little real say in the decisions that shape their lives.

## New Earth design

Governance becomes a stewardship function, not a domination structure.

Power is distributed, responsive, and transparent. Leaders are servants of the whole. Decisions are measured by their long-term impact on life, fairness, and future generations. Communities have real participatory power. Corruption becomes structurally difficult because the incentives for honesty are built in.

## Principles

- Leaders are servants of the whole, not rulers above it
- Transparency is standard; opacity is the exception
- Decisions are measured by long-term impact on life and future generations
- Communities have real participatory power in decisions that affect them
- Corruption becomes structurally difficult through distributed power and visible accountability
- Diversity and local wisdom are protected within a shared commitment to the whole

## Features

- **Local councils with real authority** — Communities govern their own affairs while respecting nested agreements
- **Regional and global cooperation bodies** — Shared planetary issues (climate, migration, trade, peace) managed through consensus-based or supermajority processes
- **Citizen assemblies** — Major long-term decisions made through deliberative democracy involving representatives from the population
- **Publicly visible budgets and decision trails** — All allocations of resources and major policy choices are documented and accessible
- **Integrity-based leadership review** — Leaders are evaluated by their wisdom, integrity, and service, not charisma or wealth
- **Separation of power** — No single person or group concentrates decision-making, budgetary, or enforcement authority

## Key questions for implementation

- How do we transition power without destabilization or chaos?
- What prevents new hierarchies or corruption from re-emerging?
- How do we balance speed of decision-making with inclusivity?
- How do we protect minority voices while honoring majority will?
- What safeguards prevent tyranny of the majority?

## Connection to other pillars

- **Economics**: Transparent budgets and public resource allocation redirect wealth toward life support
- **Education**: Teaches citizens the skills needed for meaningful participation in governance
- **Health**: Preventive health policies require long-term planning and community coordination
- **Justice**: Restorative governance addresses root causes rather than just symptoms of harm
