# 12 Family, Childhood, and Eldership

## Current distortion

Families are strained, childhood is overstimulated, and elders are often marginalized.

Parents work multiple jobs with little community support. Children are rushed, over-scheduled, and overstimulated. Emotional safety is rare. Intergenerational connection is broken. Elders are isolated in institutions, treated as burdens rather than keepers of wisdom. The knowledge and presence of eldership is lost.

## New Earth design

A healthy society protects children, supports parents, and honors elders as keepers of memory and wisdom.

Children deserve presence, safety, and guidance. Parenting is socially valuable work. Elders should remain integrated in community life, their presence and wisdom honored.

## Principles

- Children deserve presence, safety, and guidance throughout healthy development
- Parenting is socially valuable work deserving support and respect
- Families take many forms; all deserve protection and care
- Childhood should be slower, less commercialized, more rooted in nature and play
- Elders are keepers of memory, wisdom, and continuity
- Intergenerational connection and mentorship are vital to healthy communities

## Features

- **Family-supportive policies** — Paid parental leave, affordable childcare, flexible work, and economic security remove barriers to family presence
- **Community structures for parenting** — Villages, networks, and extended family arrangements reduce parental isolation
- **Slower childhood** — Protection from overstimulation, overscheduling, and commercialization; space for play, nature, rest, and developing at natural pace
- **Trauma-informed parenting and schools** — Understanding how to help children heal attachment wounds and develop secure relationships
- **Mentorship across generations** — Elders actively teach young people skills, wisdom, and cultural transmission
- **Elder councils** — Formal roles for elders in decision-making, wisdom-keeping, and community guidance
- **Intergenerational living** — Multigenerational households and communities where children, parents, and elders share life
- **Meaningful roles for later life** — Elders continue to contribute, teach, care for grandchildren, and participate in community

## Key questions for implementation

- How do we support parents without controlling family structure?
- What does healthy childhood development look like in a modern world?
- How do we heal attachment trauma across generations?
- What does elderhood look like when people are valued?
- How do we rebuild intergenerational connection?

## Connection to other pillars

- **Education**: Early childhood education and parental support shape lifelong development
- **Health and healing**: Family safety and intergenerational connection are foundational to wellbeing
- **Economics**: Parenting and elder care should be valued and supported, not hidden labor
- **Culture and spirituality**: Rites of passage and intergenerational rituals transmit meaning and belonging
