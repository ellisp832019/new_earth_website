## Locked V2 purpose

The New Earth website is the public gateway to the wider New Earth ecosystem.
It should help a visitor understand the vision, the Blueprint, the systems
thinking behind it, the practical projects emerging from it, and the ways to
learn, connect, or participate.

The website is not the internal Command Centre, Command Dashboard, GAIA
administration system, NEOS engineering interface, finance system, private AI
runtime, or repository-control environment. Those systems may be described at
an appropriate public level, but their private interfaces and operational
controls remain outside the public website scope.

## Delivery horizons and status truth

Every V2 page or capability has one delivery horizon and one status truth in the
V2 Page and Capability Register.

- `V2.0 FOUNDATION`: architecture and public foundations required first.
- `V2.x ENABLED LATER`: designed for later activation without being activated in
  WA-01.
- `EXTERNAL/INTERNAL SYSTEM`: may be explained publicly but remains operated in
  another system or private environment.
- `DEFERRED RESEARCH`: requires evidence, ownership, or research before scope.
- `NOT REQUIRED`: intentionally excluded from the public website model.

Status truth is deliberately conservative: LIVE, VALIDATED, IN DEVELOPMENT,
PROTOTYPE, RESEARCH, PLANNED, or FUTURE. Planned entries do not assert that an
unfinished product or service exists.

## Boundary rules

- The locked Blueprint remains the doctrine source of truth.
- The repository remains the structure and content source of truth.
- WordPress remains the publishing layer.
- Public ecosystem descriptions must not expose private operational systems.
- Every page must have a defined visitor need, content owner, source, route,
  accessibility expectation, and review state.
- A route change requires a redirect decision and an evidence-backed reason.
- Duplicate pages must not be merged or deleted until ownership, legal role, and
  inbound-link risk are understood.
- The main navigation should orient visitors, not expose the whole ecosystem map.

## WA-01 exit criteria

WA-01 is complete when:

- The page decision register covers every current published page.
- Proposed V2 pages and capabilities have an explicit evidence or question.
- Visitor journeys show the intended movement through the ecosystem.
- Content types and taxonomy requirements are documented.
- Duplicate, divergent, corrupted, and placeholder content is recorded.
- Open questions have owners and a next decision point.
- No live change has been made.

WA-01 is now complete. WA-02 is the next phase and will convert the approved
destinations into information architecture without silently changing live URLs.
