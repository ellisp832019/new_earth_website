## Locked planning decisions

### D01 - Public gateway boundary

**Decision:** The website explains the New Earth ecosystem but does not become an
internal operations interface.

**Source:** Direct WA-01 instruction and existing repository doctrine.

### D02 - Blueprint remains the organising doctrine

**Decision:** The Blueprint and 12 pillars remain the central conceptual
framework for future pages, projects, and public knowledge.

**Source:** 00_BLUEPRINT and 01_STRATEGY sources.

### D03 - Future capabilities are staged

**Decision:** Future-ready capabilities are represented in planning but are not
activated or published during WA-01.

**Source:** Direct WA-01 instruction.

### D04 - Evidence before route decisions

**Decision:** Duplicate or divergent live routes remain INVESTIGATE until their
content, ownership, legal role, and link risk are understood.

**Source:** WA-00B evidence and direct WA-01 instruction.
