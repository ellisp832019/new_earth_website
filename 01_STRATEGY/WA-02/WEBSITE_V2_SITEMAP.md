# Website V2 Sitemap

## Status

WA-02A information architecture proposal, 2026-08-26. Planning only. This
document does not authorize WordPress, IONOS, live-site, redirect, publishing,
design, or deployment changes.

## Architecture principles

- The website is the public gateway to the New Earth ecosystem.
- Home initially fulfils the Start Here role and previews the dedicated Vision
  page.
- Journal is the canonical publication destination. Articles is a legacy route.
- Public explanations of systems are not public applications or operational
  interfaces.
- Legal destinations sit in secondary or footer navigation.
- V2.x destinations are reserved and must not be presented as live.
- Every public V2.0 destination has one canonical URL.

## Current-live and legacy relationships

- Current live pages are represented in the WA-01 page register.
- Systems, About, Contact, and Home are rewritten rather than duplicated.
- Articles `/articles/` redirects to Journal.
- Cookie Policy (UK) `/cookie-policy-uk/` redirects to the canonical legal route.
- Blueprint Alignment remains a reusable method beneath the relevant project; it
  is not promoted to a primary top-level page.
- No additional alias is approved without evidence and a later redirect decision.

## Access and boundary model

- Public: all V2.0 pages listed above, subject to legal, evidence, rights, and
  accessibility review.
- External/internal explanation: Ecosystem system pages explain purpose and
  status, but link or refer to separate systems without absorbing them.
- Private: Command Centre operations, Command Dashboard workday functions,
  GAIA administration, NEOS engineering controls, repository control, financial
  records, credentials, and sensitive research.
