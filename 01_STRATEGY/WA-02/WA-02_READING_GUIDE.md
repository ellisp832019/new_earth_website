# WA-02 Reading Guide

## Purpose

WA-02 converts the approved WA-01 vision and content audit into a controlled
Website V2 information architecture. These documents are planning inputs only.
No website implementation has started.

## Recommended reading order

1. `WEBSITE_V2_SITEMAP.md` - hierarchy, horizons, and public/private boundary.
2. `URL_ARCHITECTURE_REGISTER.csv` - canonical routes and reserved destinations.
3. `NAVIGATION_MODEL.md` - primary, secondary, footer, mobile, and contextual navigation.
4. `PAGE_TEMPLATE_CONTENT_MODEL.md` - template families and reusable content model.
5. `SEARCH_TAXONOMY_DISCOVERY_MODEL.md` - discovery and relationships.
6. `USER_JOURNEY_IA_VALIDATION.md` - journey-by-journey IA check.
7. `COMMAND_CENTRE_DASHBOARD_BOUNDARY.md` - public explanation versus protected systems.
8. `OPEN_QUESTIONS_DECISION_LOG.md` - decisions Peter must review and later gates.

## Already locked by WA-01

- Home initially fulfils Start Here and previews a dedicated Vision page.
- Journal is canonical; Articles redirects to Journal.
- Cookie Policy (UK) redirects to the canonical legal route.
- About, Founder's Journey, Team, and creator ownership remain distinct.
- The public Command Centre information page is allowed; the private Command
  Dashboard and other operational systems remain separate.
- Status truth, legal review, WCAG 2.2 AA, image rights, progress ownership, and
  the no-private-data boundary remain locked.
- No live navigation, route, page, theme, asset, or WordPress change is included.

## WA-02B approval

Peter approved all five blocking V2.0 architecture decisions on 2026-08-26:

- Ecosystem is the public label and canonical destination; Systems receives a
  gated redirect after replacement validation.
- Get Involved is a lightweight V2.0 hub.
- Research and Development is `/research-development/` beneath Ecosystem.
- Pillar slugs have no numeric prefixes while display order remains numbered.
- New Earth Test is reserved and has a content-preservation-gated redirect to
  Inner Blueprint.

The approved header is Vision, Blueprint, Ecosystem, Projects, Journal, About,
and Get Involved. Home is reached through the New Earth logo. Footer groups are
Explore, Participate, Learn, Legal, and Trust. V2.x questions are non-blocking.
