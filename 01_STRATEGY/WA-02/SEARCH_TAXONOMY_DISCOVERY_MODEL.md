# Search, Taxonomy and Discovery Model

## Status

WA-02A planning proposal, 2026-08-26. No WordPress taxonomy or search settings
are changed.

## Journal categories

Use the existing Journal strategy as the source for a controlled category set:

- Blueprint and the 12 Pillars
- Vision and Civilizational Thinking
- Systems and Governance
- Conscious Living
- Regeneration and Ecology
- Technology and Tools
- Practical Guides
- Projects and Progress
- Research and Development

Categories describe the primary editorial purpose. A post has one primary
category; additional relationships use taxonomies or links.

## Journal tags

Tags are for narrower, reusable subjects such as a named pillar, project,
method, place, format, or recurring theme. Do not create tags that duplicate
categories, page titles, or every word in a headline. Review unused tags
regularly.

## Project and status taxonomy

Projects should support:

- Project name and canonical relationship.
- Blueprint relationship.
- Project type: product, programme, research, prototype, system, or future.
- Status truth: LIVE, VALIDATED, IN DEVELOPMENT, PROTOTYPE, RESEARCH, PLANNED,
  FUTURE.
- Owner.
- Evidence date and review state.

A status is not a marketing claim. Use the conservative label when evidence is
incomplete.

## Pillar taxonomy

Represent the twelve pillars as a controlled taxonomy or equivalent relationship
field, not twelve unrelated free-text tags. The canonical terms are Governance,
Economics, Education, Health and Healing, Food and Agriculture, Housing and
Habitat, Energy and Technology, Justice and Conflict Transformation, Media and
Information, Environment and Relationship with Nature, Culture Spirituality and
Human Development, and Family Childhood and Eldership.

## Creator lanes

Use separate ownership and relationship terms for:

- Peter Practical Guides.
- Hayley Conscious Living.
- Shared Founder's Journey.
- Team and responsibilities.

Do not place Hayley content in her name without her approval. Do not treat
creator lanes as generic Journal categories.

## Relationships

A content item may relate to:

- One or more Blueprint pillars.
- One project or system.
- One primary creator lane where approved.
- One Journal category.
- Related resources, FAQ terms, or legal context.

Relationships should be explicit and bidirectional where useful. They must not
create duplicate canonical URLs.

## V2.0 search scope

Search should cover published public pages and Journal content, with:

- Clear result type.
- Title, summary, and context.
- Canonical URL.
- Accessible empty, loading, and error states.
- Exclusion of drafts, private systems, credentials, and internal records.
- No duplicate Articles index.
- Research and Development is discoverable under the Ecosystem relationship;
  private research and unpublished material remain excluded.

## Future V2.x search

Reserve filtering for project status, pillar, creator lane, media type, and
evidence date when content volume and governance justify it. Do not delay V2.0
for advanced federated search, protected portal search, or external system
indexing.

## Governance

Every taxonomy needs an owner, definition, review rule, and migration note.
Avoid uncontrolled synonyms such as Articles/Journal, project/product used
interchangeably, or multiple labels for the same pillar.
