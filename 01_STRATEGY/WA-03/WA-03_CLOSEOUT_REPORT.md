## Preview and validation

The offline preview is at
`01_STRATEGY/WA-03/PREVIEW/design-system-preview.html`. It presents Direction
C first and as the default, while retaining labelled A/B comparison material.
It has no network, script, tracker, or external-font dependency and includes
focus, mobile, and reduced-motion review paths.

The remaining work is non-blocking implementation validation: final rendered
token values, component states and WordPress pattern granularity, page-template
hero assignments, final icon/diagram choices, breakpoint tuning, any

The approved external application standard covers LinkedIn, YouTube, Telegram,
GitHub, newsletter/email providers, donations/support services, and future
public applications. It requires canonical New Earth ownership, labelled
accessible links, consent-controlled embeds where justified, fallbacks, and
privacy/performance/security review. The preview uses safe placeholders only;
it loads no third-party scripts, feeds, videos, or trackers.
