# Current Visual Baseline Audit

## Status

WA-03A design-system planning baseline, 2026-08-26. This is a constructive,
repository-based audit. No theme, content, asset, WordPress, or live-site change
is authorized.

## Existing identity

The current identity is calm, grounded, intelligent, spiritual but clear, and
practical rather than corporate. Brand and strategy documents position New Earth
as a living framework for conscious civilization, with MicroGrow as its first
practical product expression.

The existing child theme and design notes establish:

- Earth green, dark green, warm sand, soft off-white, brown, muted gold, black
  text, and soft text.
- A restrained radius system and soft shadow.
- Playfair Display-style serif headings with an Inter-style sans body approach.
- A 760px reading width and 1200px wide layout.
- Simple block-theme compatibility and maintainable code.

## Assets and imagery

Nine normalized production heroes are registered and present, primarily 1920x1080
WebP, with contact at 1600x900. Their direction is cinematic, earth-centred,
peaceful, regenerative, and spacious enough for text overlays. Reference PNGs
remain archival/unapproved until rights and usage policy are confirmed.

The strongest visual opportunity is the connection between landscape, human
purpose, practical projects, and systems thinking. Imagery should support
orientation and trust, not make research or internal technology appear
production-ready.

## Strengths to preserve

- Distinctive earth-centred palette.
- Warm editorial contrast between serif expression and readable sans text.
- Existing hero investment and normalized dimensions.
- Calm visual tone rather than generic corporate technology.
- Simple maintainable child-theme foundation.
- Reusable block and asset workflow.
- Clear separation between public explanations and private operations.

## Consistency and usability weaknesses

- The current baseline contains multiple navigation entities and an overcrowded
  flat header/footer relationship.
- The current visual tokens are not yet documented as a single approved system.
- Status, evidence, ownership, and review information need consistent components.
- The architecture now spans doctrine, projects, research, creator lanes, legal,
  and public system explanations; current patterns do not yet define all of them.
- Hero overlays, long-form reading, mobile crops, and focus states need explicit
  rules.
- Gold, serif display type, and imagery need contrast and restraint rules.

## Risks

- Low-contrast gold or soft text can reduce readability.
- Large cinematic images can obscure headings on small screens.
- Decorative effects could make the public site resemble a private dashboard.
- Unapproved reference imagery could be mistaken for publishable media.
- Remote fonts or scripts would introduce privacy, resilience, and performance
  dependencies.
- Colour alone must not communicate project status or system safety.

## Architecture gap

WA-02 establishes 38 public V2.0 destinations, a concise header, grouped footer,
public Command Centre explanation, and reserved future capabilities. The visual
system must therefore scale from doctrine and pillars to projects, Journal,
research, creator lanes, legal pages, and safe technology explainers without
creating separate visual languages.

## Retain, refine, replace

### Retain

Earth-centred colours, warm editorial tone, normalized production heroes,
logo clarity, accessible link underlines, maintainable block-theme foundation,
and calm spacious presentation.

### Refine

Tokenize colour and typography, define a responsive spacing rhythm, standardize
hero crops and focal points, add status/evidence components, formalize card and
CTA patterns, and add WCAG 2.2 AA acceptance rules.

### Replace or avoid

Overcrowded navigation, ad hoc page-specific styling, unbounded gold accents,
remote font dependencies, dashboard-like dense data panels, and visual claims
that imply unverified readiness or private operational access.
