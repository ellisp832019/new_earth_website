# WA-04 Open Questions and Decision Log

## Resolved for WA-04A

- Global shell follows the approved WA-02 header and footer architecture.
- Direction C - Earth and Future is the only visual direction.
- Homepage primary action is Explore the Blueprint; secondary action is See
  Projects.
- The twelve pillars use the approved descriptive URLs and display order.
- External integrations are placeholders or normal links only.
- The prototype is offline and JavaScript is progressive enhancement.
- Existing approved hero assets may provide contextual imagery, but their
  page-specific suitability and rights remain subject to later review.
- A dedicated MicroGrow homepage project-card asset remains required; its page
  hero must not be reused in that card.
- Homepage image-led cards use one reusable one-third media/two-thirds writing
  contract with visible status, title, explanation, and action.
- New Earth Builders is approved for a normal labelled external link in the
  offline preview as an independent community platform. Courtesy review remains
  required before public deployment; no partnership, ownership, embed, feed,
  tracker, API connection, or account integration is claimed.
