# New Earth Website — WA-04 Homepage Direction

Status: APPROVED
Approved by: Peter
Phase: WA-04 — Homepage

## Homepage Narrative

Approved visitor journey:

Purpose
→ Practical evidence
→ Ecosystem understanding
→ Learn
→ Digital Products
→ Journal
→ People
→ Participation

## Approved Homepage Sections

1. Purpose / Hero
2. What New Earth Means in Practice
3. What We're Building
4. One Connected Ecosystem
5. Learn
6. Digital Products
7. Journal / Building in Public
8. The People Behind New Earth
9. Participate / Contact

## Hero

The hero must answer:

- What is New Earth?
- Why should I care?

Primary CTA:

See what we're building
→ /what-were-building/

Secondary CTA:

Explore the vision
→ /vision/

The hero must not attempt to explain the full internal ecosystem.

## Purpose / Self-Sovereignty

The homepage should explain self-sovereignty in plain language through:

- knowledge
- capability
- informed choice
- meaningful human control
- responsibility

Avoid ideological or political framing.

## Company Boundary

New Earth:
the wider vision and body of work.

New Earth Advanced Technologies Ltd:
the operating company for selected technology, research, engineering and product work.

The company must not be presented as owner of the entire New Earth ecosystem.

## What We're Building

Homepage featured evidence-bearing project records:

- MicroGrow
- New Earth Command Centre

MicroGrow remains subject to CF-02 before public release.

Other named systems remain contextual or evidence-bounded until separately approved.

R&D appears as a compact supporting context within What We're Building rather than as a standalone homepage section.

## Ecosystem Preview

Homepage uses the Simple Visitor Model rather than the complete ecosystem architecture.

The homepage preview should explain approximately five high-level relationships:

- shared purpose/framework
- practical projects/research
- knowledge/creator work
- approved products/tools
- people/cooperation

It must:

- work without JavaScript
- stack clearly on mobile
- include a complete text equivalent
- avoid implying ownership or completed integration

The deeper Ecosystem experience will contain the fuller public architecture.

## Learn

Homepage Learn should initially use useful existing material.

Approved starting points:

- Blueprint
- Inner Blueprint
- New Earth in Practice

The homepage must not promote Learn as a full learning platform.

The /learn/ route must satisfy the approved minimum-useful-content gate before public activation.

## Digital Products

Homepage product emphasis:

1. AI Made Simple
2. The Fifth Dimension, Lad

AI Made Simple:
lead Digital Product feature.

It remains a required next-release product target and remains readiness/activation gated.

The Fifth Dimension, Lad:
compact editorial Coming Soon teaser.

It must not imply current:
- purchase
- donation
- fulfilment
- download
- launch date

Both should link to durable approved product/detail destinations when those routes are ready.

## Journal / Building in Public

Journal remains the single dated publishing destination.

When Journal is empty:
do not render fake article cards.

When populated:
show approved dated entries only.

Progress, Updates, Research Notes and Building in Public should not remain as competing homepage archives.

Any historical progress item requires separate approval before conversion into a Journal record.

## People / Company

Homepage should briefly introduce:

- Peter
- Hayley
- company context

Use existing approved portrait material where suitable.

Primary people CTA:

Meet Peter and Hayley
→ /team/

Do not turn the homepage into an About page.

## Participation / Contact

Final homepage actions should remain simple.

Primary routes may include:

- Explore ways to contribute
- Start a conversation
- Read the Journal when entries exist

Do not expose all contact form categories as homepage actions.

Do not imply:
- membership
- donation service
- investment offer
- newsletter subscription

unless separately implemented and approved.

## Homepage Visual Rhythm

Approved rhythm:

- atmospheric hero
- quiet text-led purpose/company section
- evidence-led practical work feature
- ecosystem/framework composition
- readable Learn rows
- cover-led Digital Products section
- dated Journal list
- portrait/text people section
- restrained participation/contact band

Avoid:
card grid after card grid after card grid.
