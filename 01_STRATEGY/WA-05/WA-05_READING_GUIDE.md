controlled terminology, source register, and V2.0 content inventory. WA-05B

## How to use the records

Assign every claim an evidence class before drafting. Use the terminology
register to keep public labels consistent. Use the page inventory for scope and
readiness, not as page copy. Draft in the production sequence and stop at the
listed owner, technical, legal, or partner gate.

## Boundaries

WA-05A does not rewrite pages, publish content, change WordPress, alter the live
site, activate external integrations, expose private systems, or prove claims
from other repositories. Platform Core, NEOS, GAIA, Local AI Runtime, and
protected Dashboard details remain evidence dependencies.

New Earth Builders remains an independent external platform. MicroGrow AI Lab
is research-stage; Field Scanner is a device concept; New Earth Living is an
app direction; and future intent is not availability.
