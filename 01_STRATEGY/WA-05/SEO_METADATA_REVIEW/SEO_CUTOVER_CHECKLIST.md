# SEO Cutover Checklist

- Inventory every legacy URL before migration; do not rely on the route register alone.
- Confirm preferred HTTPS host, canonicals, internal links, assets and redirects.
- Generate and validate the new sitemap and production robots policy.
- Test HTTP/HTTPS and www/non-www behaviour, redirect chains and 404s.
- Preserve high-value inbound paths and avoid redirecting unrelated URLs to Home.
- Confirm noindex is absent from intended production pages and present on protected staging.
- Verify representative pages, mobile rendering, social cards and structured data.
- Connect and verify Search Console only in a separately authorised launch task.
- Monitor crawl errors, canonical selection, indexing, Core Web Vitals and security signals.
- Keep useful permanent redirects for as long as they transfer value and remain useful.
