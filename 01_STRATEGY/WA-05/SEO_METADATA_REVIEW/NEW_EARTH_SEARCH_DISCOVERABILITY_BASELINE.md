# New Earth Search Discoverability Baseline

**WEBSITE-DERIVED SEARCH/DISCOVERABILITY BASELINE**

- Prefer people-first, useful and distinctive New Earth content.
- Use real evidence, original diagrams, clear entities and transparent maturity.
- Preserve semantic headings, crawlability, accessible content and meaningful images.
- Build logical internal links between vision, principles, Blueprint, Pillars,
  ecosystem, projects and Journal.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, mass low-value AI pages, fake GEO/AEO techniques,
  fabricated authority, ratings, reviews or rankings.
- Keep future systems and commerce separate from current informational content.

This may inform future content systems but is not a cross-repository requirement.
