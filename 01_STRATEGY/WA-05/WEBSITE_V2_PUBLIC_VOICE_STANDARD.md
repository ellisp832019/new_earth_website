# Website V2 Public Voice Standard

## Voice

New Earth speaks in a grounded, warm, clear, hopeful, intelligent, practical,
sincere, human, non-corporate, and non-preachy voice. It welcomes newcomers,
respects spiritual language without presenting belief as established fact, and
is honest about uncertainty and development status.

## Writing rules

- Prefer short, active sentences with one clear idea at a time.
- Explain unfamiliar terms before using them repeatedly.
- Use concrete verbs: explore, learn, test, connect, build, reflect, and share.
- Prefer "could", "is exploring", and "is being developed" when evidence is incomplete.
- Use headings that tell visitors what a section helps them understand.
- Use CTAs that describe the next action: Read the Manifesto, Explore the Blueprint, or Contact New Earth.
- Describe projects by purpose, current status, owner, and evidence boundary.
- Write for a first-time visitor, not for an internal delivery team.

Avoid inflated, corporate, coercive, or absolute language such as revolutionary,
guaranteed, proven, ready for everyone, world-changing, the solution, or join
the movement. Avoid preaching, fear, shame, insider shorthand, unexplained
acronyms, and claims of certainty about spiritual or future outcomes.

## Public versus internal language

Do not expose phrases such as approved structure, canonical destination,
production blocker, evidence-led page work, representative card, controlled
input, or internal system status. Translate them into visitor language: where a
page belongs, what is still being developed, what is known, and what a visitor
can do next.

## Voice boundaries

Founder voice may use first person for an identified creator's approved story,
experience, or reflection. Institutional voice speaks for New Earth as a whole
and must not present an individual's belief, intention, or experience as an
established organisational fact. Peter's and Hayley's creator lanes remain
distinct and require their respective approval.

## Examples

Approved: "MicroGrow AI Lab is exploring how local growing systems and
intelligence could work together."

Rejected: "MicroGrow AI Lab is a proven autonomous food system ready to deploy."

Approved: "The Blueprint offers a way to explore the relationships between the
12 Pillars."

Rejected: "The Blueprint is the guaranteed plan for a better civilisation."

Approved: "New Earth Builders is an independent community platform."

Rejected: "New Earth partners with and operates New Earth Builders."

## Accessibility and plain language

Use meaningful link text, logical heading order, descriptive image alternatives,
plain UK English, readable paragraphs, and text alternatives for diagrams.
Do not place essential meaning only in images, colour, animation, or technical
language. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and
reduced-motion behaviour.
