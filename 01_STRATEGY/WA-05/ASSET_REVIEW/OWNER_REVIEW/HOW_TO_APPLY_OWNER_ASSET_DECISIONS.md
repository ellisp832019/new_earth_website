# Applying Owner Asset Decisions

1. Launch the review tool.
2. Review every candidate.
3. Choose `KEEP`, `REPLACE`, `HOLD` or `DON'T USE`.
4. Add notes where useful.
5. Export the CSV.
6. Provide the exported CSV for Codex validation.
7. Codex validates and imports the decisions into governed records.
8. `KEEP` proceeds to targeted rights/provenance checks.
9. `REPLACE` proceeds to fresh controlled asset production.
10. `HOLD` remains outside the public build.
11. `DON'T USE` remains reference/archive only.
12. No image becomes public solely because it received owner visual approval.
