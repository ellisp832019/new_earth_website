# Fast Replacement Policy

If a desired asset cannot obtain sufficiently clear provenance without
disproportionate effort:

1. Retain the original as local reference.
2. Mark it `NOT FOR PUBLIC BUILD`.
3. Use the visual idea only as inspiration where appropriate.
4. Create or commission a fresh controlled replacement.
5. Record provenance immediately.
6. Process the replacement through accessibility and rights governance.
7. Promote only after approval.

Unclear historical provenance must not hold V2 indefinitely when a controlled
replacement is practical.
