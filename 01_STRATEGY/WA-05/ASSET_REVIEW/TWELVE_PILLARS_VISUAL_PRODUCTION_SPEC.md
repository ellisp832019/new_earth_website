# 12 Pillars Visual Production Specification

- Use a coherent but distinguishable visual family with stable, URL-safe names.
- Retain original masters; create derivatives during build rather than overwriting.
- Define landscape hero, card and social aspect families before production.
- Keep focal subjects inside a mobile/social safe area and avoid essential text in pixels.
- Classify each use as decorative, informative, functional, complex, text-in-image or logo/brand.
- Write alt text for purpose, not keywords; provide text equivalents for diagrams.
- Capture source, creator, generation method/date, rights evidence and restrictions.
- Review maturity and claims so a concept is not presented as an operating system or product.
- Coordinate with the accessible Pillar and SEO templates; do not rely on colour alone.
