# System Page Asset Specification

For MicroGrow, GAIA, NEOS, Platform Core, Command Centre, New Earth Living, BioCalm
and CKCC, record maturity, evidence gate, provenance, rights, caption and alt text.
Screenshots require privacy and rights review. Diagrams require text equivalents.
Do not show conceptual renders as physical products, imply health certification or
expose protected architecture. Technical captions must follow WA-05C.
