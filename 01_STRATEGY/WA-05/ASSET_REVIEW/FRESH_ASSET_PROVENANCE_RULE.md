# Fresh Asset Provenance Rule

Every newly created Website V2 asset must record its asset ID, creation date,
creator/operator, source method, tool/provider, reference inputs, rights/terms
evidence, human editing, third-party elements, intended route and role,
accessibility classification, owner approval, provenance review and public-build
approval.

This applies particularly to 12 Pillars, ecosystem and system diagrams, Journal
imagery, Peter and Hayley creator assets, and social/Open Graph images. AI-assisted
creation is not automatic rights clearance.
