# Public Claims and Evidence Standard

Every public statement receives one evidence class before drafting. The owner
reviews sensitive claims and records the next review date. The examples below
are controls, not permission to claim that a page or project exists.

| Class | Meaning | Minimum evidence | Permitted wording | Prohibited wording | Owner | Review |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ESTABLISHED FACT | Verified current fact | Direct authoritative record | "is", with scope | Unqualified extrapolation | Relevant owner | Annual or on change |
| DOCUMENTED HISTORY | Supported past event | Dated source or record | "was", "in [date]" | Present-tense assumption | New Earth / source owner | Annual |
| VISION | Desired future direction | Approved scope or vision | "we envision", "could" | Promise or guarantee | New Earth | Per phase |
| PRINCIPLE | Chosen value or rule | Approved doctrine | "we aim to", "we believe" | Universal proof claim | New Earth | Annual |
| RESEARCH | Investigation in progress | Named question and owner | "exploring", "researching" | Proven result | Research owner | Per milestone |
| CONCEPT | Early idea or representation | Approved concept record | "concept", "could" | Product or availability claim | Project owner | Per review |
| PROTOTYPE | Experimental implementation | Prototype evidence and scope | "prototype", "testing" | Production-ready claim | Project owner | Per test |
| IN DEVELOPMENT | Being built or prepared | Owner and current work record | "in development" | Available now | Delivery owner | Monthly or milestone |
| PILOT | Limited, bounded trial | Defined participants, scope, result record | "pilot", with limits | Generalised outcome | Pilot owner | Per phase |
| AVAILABLE | Publicly obtainable now | Verified access and support | "available at" | Implied universal access | Service owner | Before publication and quarterly |
| PARTNER-PROVIDED | Supplied by an external owner | Permission, source, and date | "provided by", with attribution | New Earth ownership claim | Partner and New Earth | On change |
| COMING LATER | Intentionally deferred | Approved future route | "coming later", "planned" | Implied launch date | New Earth | Per phase |
| UNKNOWN / REQUIRES VERIFICATION | Not established | Open evidence dependency | "not yet verified" | Guessing or certainty | Assigned reviewer | Before use |

Concept is not a product. Research is not a validated outcome. A prototype is
not production-ready. In development is not available. Future intent is not a
promise. An external link is not an integration. An informal relationship is
not a formal partnership.

AI must not be described as controlling safety-critical physical systems unless
that behaviour is independently verified, bounded, and specifically approved.
Public descriptions must not expose credentials, private infrastructure,
personal records, or unverified technical details.
