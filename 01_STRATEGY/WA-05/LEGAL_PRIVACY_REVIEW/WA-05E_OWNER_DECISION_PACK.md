# WA-05E Owner Decision Pack

This pack records only decisions that cannot be answered from the current
repository evidence. It does not approve legal wording or activate any service.

## Before Website V2 Publication

### OD-001 - Which legal entity operates the website?

**Why this matters**

The website must identify its operator and provide accurate company disclosures.

**What we currently know**

The repository does not verify the registered company name, number, jurisdiction,
registered office or controller contact details.

**Decision needed**

Confirm the legal operator and the information authorised for public disclosure.

**Options**

- Confirm a UK limited company and provide verified Companies House details.
- Confirm another operator and provide its verified legal details.
- Hold publication until the operator is confirmed.

**Recommended default**

Hold legal/company disclosure publication until verified details are supplied.

**Can Website V2 publish before this is decided?**

NO for a final public launch.

### OD-002 - What contact route should the public site use?

**Why this matters**

Contact and Get Involved currently describe routes but do not define a form,
mailbox, fields, retention or recipient.

**What we currently know**

No form implementation or field specification is evidenced.

**Decision needed**

Choose a minimal contact route and whether a form is needed at all.

**Options**

- Publish a monitored contact address with a clear notice.
- Add a minimal form after data-flow and privacy design.
- Keep contact invitation deferred.

**Recommended default**

Use the least-data route that meets the immediate purpose.

**Can Website V2 publish before this is decided?**

YES, if no collection is activated and the public copy remains accurate.

## Before Commerce

### OD-003 - Is commerce in scope for this V2 release?

**Why this matters**

Selling guides, products, memberships or support activates consumer, payment and
privacy obligations.

**What we currently know**

No shop, checkout, prices, payment or download flow is present in the V2 evidence.

**Decision needed**

Confirm whether commerce is deferred from V2.

**Recommended default**

Keep the current release informational and defer commerce.

**Can Website V2 publish before this is decided?**

YES, without commerce mechanisms or sales claims.

## Before Community

### OD-004 - Will New Earth operate a native community service?

**Why this matters**

Profiles, posts, messaging or uploads require moderation, safety, privacy and
retention design.

**What we currently know**

The current Builders route is an independent external platform link; native UGC
is not evidenced.

**Decision needed**

Confirm whether native community functionality is future-only.

**Recommended default**

Keep native community functionality out of V2 and label external destinations.

**Can Website V2 publish before this is decided?**

YES, with external-link treatment only.

## Before App or Personal-Data Activation

### OD-005 - Which future data-bearing capabilities should proceed first?

**Why this matters**

AI, wellbeing, device and account features can involve sensitive data and require
separate technical, safety and privacy review.

**What we currently know**

New Earth Living, BioCalm and GAIA are programme context leads, not current public
services in the Website V2 evidence.

**Decision needed**

Select a future capability for a separate activation brief, if any.

**Recommended default**

Do not activate a data-bearing capability from the informational website baseline.

**Can Website V2 publish before this is decided?**

YES, provided no such capability is presented as available.
