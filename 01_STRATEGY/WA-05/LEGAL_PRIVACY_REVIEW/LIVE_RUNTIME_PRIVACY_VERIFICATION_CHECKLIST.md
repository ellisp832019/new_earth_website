# Live Runtime Privacy Verification Checklist

Future execution checklist. Do not treat this document as evidence that any
runtime inspection has occurred.

- Confirm the live operator, controller, company disclosures and privacy links.
- Inspect browser cookies, local storage, session storage and other identifiers.
- Record cookie names, purposes, lifetimes, domains and whether each is necessary.
- Inspect all scripts, tags, pixels, fonts, embeds, widgets and network calls.
- Identify analytics, advertising, CAPTCHA, video, maps, email and payment providers.
- Verify consent, withdrawal, objection and preference controls where required.
- Test that blocked third-party content does not load before the relevant choice.
- Test external-link labels, destination notices and fallback states.
- Inspect contact and Get Involved forms, fields, notices, recipients and errors.
- Confirm whether form submissions reach email, a plugin, a CRM or another service.
- Confirm marketing opt-in is separate from service/contact communication.
- Verify unsubscribe, suppression and consent records for any newsletter.
- Verify mobile behaviour, keyboard access, focus visibility and readable notices.
- Check privacy, cookie, terms and disclaimer links from header/footer/legal pages.
- Record hosting, transfer and processor evidence without exposing secrets.
- Check AI, wellbeing, device, commerce, community and child-facing features for
  accidental activation or misleading availability claims.
- Preserve screenshots or logs only after redacting personal and secret values.
