# Website V2 Publication Scope Baseline

## Purpose

This document converts the WA-05E1 owner direction into a controlled initial
launch boundary. It authorises continued planning and specialist review, not
publication or deployment.

## Proven WA-05 Baseline

- Six core pages are owner approved and remain `OWNER APPROVED - SPECIALIST REVIEW PENDING`.
- WA-05C technical review remains `PASS WITH QUALIFICATIONS`.
- WA-05D founder attribution remains `PASS / NOT REQUIRED`.
- WA-05E legal/privacy readiness remains action-led; no legal approval is claimed.
- `/manifesto/` is canonical and the V2 route inventory contains 39 unique routes.

## Initial V2 Definition

**Website V2.0 is an informational public gateway to the New Earth ecosystem.**

## Included at Initial Launch

- Vision, Manifesto, Blueprint and Ecosystem explanation.
- Verified founder/company positioning only.
- Appropriately qualified systems, projects and research descriptions.
- The 12 Pillars as clearly labelled vision, principle, research or planned work.
- Journal and editorial routes.
- Clearly labelled external resources and communities.
- A minimal verified business contact route when available.

## Explicitly Deferred

Commerce, checkout, payments, subscriptions, memberships, fulfilment, donations,
investment, native profiles, forums, messaging, uploads, newsletter activation,
user accounts, AI chat, GAIA interaction, wellbeing data, BioCalm data, device
accounts, cloud backup and MicroGrow customer accounts.

## Minimal Contact Model

Prefer a verified New Earth business contact route over a large native form. Do
not invent an email address. Until verified, record `PUBLIC CONTACT ROUTE - OWNER
VERIFICATION REQUIRED`. Any future form requires a separate data-flow decision.

## Company Disclosure Verification

The intended operator lead is New Earth Advanced Technologies Ltd, pending exact
authoritative verification of name, number, jurisdiction, registered office and
status. No exact values are recorded here.

## Technical Claim Boundary

Technical maturity must follow owning-lane evidence. Public descriptions must
distinguish concept, research, prototype, active development and operational
capability. Technical evidence wins over programme assumption.

## Founder Attribution Boundary

Current drafts contain no Hayley-specific or shared-founder attribution. Reopen
founder review before adding personal history, attributed voice or creator-specific
claims.

## Local-First / Privacy Claim Boundary

Local-first and privacy-conscious architecture are directions, not guarantees.
Do not promise complete privacy, no tracking, total security, anonymity or that
data never leaves a device without service-specific evidence.

## AI Boundary

GAIA and other AI concepts must not be presented as available, infallible,
autonomous or professional medical, legal, financial or safety-critical advice.

## Health / Wellbeing Boundary

New Earth Living and BioCalm remain future wellbeing, health, device and privacy
triggers. Do not imply diagnosis, treatment, therapeutic efficacy or medical status.

## MicroGrow Boundary

Keep MicroGrow bounded between design, bench evidence, prototype, physical
validation, production, certification and commercial availability. Preserve the
AI Lab boundary against unrestricted autonomous actuator control.

## Creator Lane Boundary

Peter Practical Guides and New Earth - Conscious Living are future creator lanes.
Do not imply products, prices, sales or availability in the initial informational site.

## Community Boundary

Native community functionality is deferred. External communities may be linked as
external services and must not appear to be controlled by New Earth.

## Commerce Boundary

Commerce is deferred. No shop, checkout, payment, subscription, donation or
investment flow is authorised in the initial V2.

## Data-Bearing Capability Boundary

Do not deliberately activate accounts, newsletter, AI interaction, wellbeing or
device data, payment data, community registration or funding applications.
Runtime processing by hosting and browser technologies still requires verification.

## 12 Pillars Boundary

The 12 Pillars may be produced as public content only with explicit maturity
labels. They are not automatically operational products.

## Runtime Verification Boundary

Before launch, verify cookies, storage, scripts, requests, forms, analytics,
embeds, fonts, CAPTCHA, consent, privacy links, external services and mobile behaviour.

## Future Activation Gates

The WA-05E future-gates register applies before digital products, creator sales,
newsletter, payments, accounts, community, AI, wellbeing, BioCalm, MicroGrow
sales, children/youth, donations, investment or international commerce.

## Conditions For Expanding V2

Expansion requires a defined capability, evidence owner, owner decision, technical
and legal/privacy review, accessible public wording, rights review and a documented
activation gate. Future intent is not current availability.
