# Core Page Editorial Review

## Review sequence

1. Check the page against the authority, voice, terminology, and claims standards.
2. Verify every factual statement against its recorded source and evidence class.
3. Read the page aloud for warmth, clarity, rhythm, and newcomer accessibility.
4. Confirm that headings and calls to action describe a useful next step.
5. Check internal links against the approved WA-02 canonical routes.
6. Review images, alternatives, captions, rights, and text alternatives.
7. Escalate founder, partner, legal, health, safety, company, financial, and technical claims.
8. Record owner approval, date, status, dependencies, and next review.

## Page-specific review

- Vision: keep invitation and framework distinct from certainty or completion.
- Manifesto: keep principles inspiring but non-dogmatic and non-grandiose.
- Blueprint: explain structure without presenting a universal finished plan.
- Ecosystem: explain relationships without exposing private systems or claiming integrations.
- About: verify origin, stewardship, people, and company wording before publication.
- Get Involved: preserve Learn, Contribute, Collaborate, and Support; describe New Earth Builders as independent and external.
