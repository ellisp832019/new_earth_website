## Page purpose

This page explains the relationships within New Earth in public, plain language.
It helps visitors understand the whole without exposing protected applications
or making claims about unverified technical capability.

## Who this is for

New visitors who want an overview, and people trying to understand how the
Blueprint, the 12 Pillars, projects, research and participation relate.

## The relationship model

The vision gives New Earth its direction. The [Blueprint](/blueprint/) offers a
structure for exploring that direction. The [12 Pillars](/blueprint/pillars/)
connect the framework with major areas of life. Projects explore how ideas might
be applied, while the [Journal](/journal/) can hold questions, learning and
documented progress.

People bring the ecosystem to life through attention, conversation, contribution
and careful collaboration. The [Get Involved](/get-involved/) page offers clear
ways to begin.

## Public and protected boundaries

The public [Command Centre information page](/ecosystem/command-centre/) can
explain purpose and context. Private dashboards, operational controls and
authenticated applications remain separate protected systems. This page does
not claim technical integration or operational capacity that has not been
independently verified.
