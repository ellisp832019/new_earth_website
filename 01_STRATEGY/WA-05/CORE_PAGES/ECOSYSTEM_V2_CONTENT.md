## Local-first principle

**New Earth starts where we are.** The model begins with people taking greater
responsibility for their own piece of Earth - their wellbeing, homes, food,
knowledge, resources, environment and community. From there, local capability
can grow and communities can connect, cooperate and share what works.

**Local first does not mean isolated.** Strong communities can remain connected
to one another, sharing knowledge, tools, resources and support while retaining
the ability to shape their own healthy future.

## A public relationship map

The vision gives New Earth its direction. The [Blueprint](/blueprint/) offers a
structure for exploring that direction. The [12 Pillars](/blueprint/pillars/)

be applied, while the [Journal](/journal/) can hold questions, learning and

People bring the ecosystem to life through attention, conversation, contribution

ways to begin.

## Technology in service of local capability

Technology should strengthen human and community capability rather than
unnecessarily remove knowledge, agency or control from the people who rely on
it. MicroGrow explores local food and growing capability; GAIA explores local-
first assistance; NEOS explores understanding and maintaining technical
systems; the New Earth Dashboard helps organise day-to-day work and resources;
and the Command Centre provides context for coordinating approved systems while
keeping people in control. Knowledge and guides help make useful learning more
available locally. These descriptions reflect areas of development and
exploration, not claims that every capability is production-ready.

## Local stewardship and connected communities

New Earth is not merely a philosophy, a technology company or a centralised
system intended to control communities. It is a framework in which people and
communities strengthen their own capacity, stewardship and resilience, supported
by appropriate systems, knowledge, technology and wider cooperation.

The sequence is local communities, connection, sharing and cooperation toward a
wider New Earth. Communities may share knowledge, tools, experience, resources,
learning, proven approaches and mutual support without a central authority
coordinating them all.
