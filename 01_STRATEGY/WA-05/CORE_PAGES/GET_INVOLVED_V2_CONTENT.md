# Get Involved

**Canonical URL:** `/get-involved/`
**Search title:** Get Involved with New Earth | Learn, Contribute and Collaborate
**Meta description:** Find a clear way to engage with New Earth through learning, contribution, collaboration or practical support.

## Page purpose

This page gives visitors safe, understandable routes into New Earth. It does not
promise membership, outcomes, funding arrangements, access to protected systems
or a particular role.

## Who this is for

People who want to learn, offer time or knowledge, explore collaboration, or
follow the development of New Earth work.

## Hero

**Eyebrow:** Find your way to take part

**Hero introduction:** Start with understanding, then choose a route that fits
your interest and capacity. Participation can begin with a question, a useful
conversation or the time to learn.

## Learn

Explore the [Vision](/vision/), [Manifesto](/manifesto/), [Blueprint](/blueprint/)
and [Journal](/journal/). These routes provide context before you decide what
you would like to do next.

## Contribute

Share knowledge, experience, time or practical support where it is appropriate.
Contribution will take different forms as projects and publishing develop. Use
the [Contact](/contact/) route to start a clear conversation.

## Collaborate

Explore aligned conversations and carefully defined work through
[Collaboration](/collaborate/). New Earth does not imply a formal partnership,
endorsement or shared governance without specific evidence and approval.

## Support

Follow the work, share useful learning and look for future ways to support
projects as they develop. No donation or funding offer is implied by this draft.

## New Earth Builders

New Earth Builders is an independent community platform. An intentional
community connecting innovators, healers, inventors and creators who want to
collaborate, exchange knowledge and help bring New Earth-aligned projects into
being. Visit [New Earth Builders](https://new-earth-builders.com/) to explore
its own public space. This is a normal external link, not an embedded service
or technical integration.
