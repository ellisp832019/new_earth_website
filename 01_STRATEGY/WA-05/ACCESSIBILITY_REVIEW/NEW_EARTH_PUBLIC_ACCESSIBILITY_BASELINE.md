# New Earth Public Accessibility Baseline

**WEBSITE-DERIVED CANDIDATE BASELINE FOR FUTURE ECOSYSTEM UI/UX REVIEW**

This is a candidate handoff from the Website V2 lane. It does not govern other
repositories or claim that any wider New Earth interface conforms to WCAG.

- Clear hierarchy and plain-language orientation.
- Keyboard-first operation with obvious visible focus.
- Consistent navigation and predictable controls.
- Status communicated with text and not colour alone.
- Low cognitive load, progressive disclosure and restrained motion.
- Readable text, clear labels and meaningful link purpose.
- Accessible errors, status messages and recovery paths.
- Responsive layouts that support zoom and reflow.
- Meaningful textual equivalents for diagrams and media.
- User agency: no hidden critical actions, forced motion or surprise changes.
- Current capability, research, future intent and operational state remain distinct.

These principles may inform future UI/UX coherence review after separate approval.
