Describe the human or ecological question without claiming a guaranteed outcome.

## The Current Challenge

## New Earth's Principle

Separate principle from proven capability.

## What New Earth Is Exploring / Building

Use an explicit maturity label: vision, principle, research, proposal, planned
programme, prototype, active development or proven capability.

## How It Connects To The Wider Ecosystem

Explain relationships in text and link only to approved routes.

## Diagram / Visual

Provide purpose-focused alt text, an adjacent equivalent text explanation and
labels that do not depend on colour, hover or animation.

## Current Status / Maturity

Use a truthful dated status. Do not turn a future system into a current product.

## What Comes Next

Describe the next controlled step, not a promised outcome.

## Related Pillars

Use descriptive link text and do not rely on visual position alone.

## Related New Earth Systems

Name only evidenced systems and explain any unfamiliar acronym at first use.

## Further Reading / Evidence

Link to authoritative repository-approved sources and label external destinations.

## Production Rules

- Use one H1 followed by logical H2/H3 headings.
- Keep summaries short, direct and suitable for newcomers.
- Use meaningful link text; avoid bare `Read more` links without context.
- Define acronyms such as GAIA, NEOS or CKCC when first introduced.
- Describe images honestly; mark decorative images for assistive technology.
- Provide text equivalents for complex diagrams and never use colour alone.
- Keep current, planned, research and operational states distinct.
- Use predictable CTAs and avoid unnecessary choice or jargon.
- Test keyboard access, focus, zoom, reflow, screen-reader order and contrast.
