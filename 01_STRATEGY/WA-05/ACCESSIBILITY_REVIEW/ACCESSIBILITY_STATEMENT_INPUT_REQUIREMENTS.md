# Accessibility Statement Input Requirements

Do not publish a conformance statement from this repository-only review. Do not
state that public-sector accessibility regulations apply without a separate legal
determination.

Before a voluntary public statement is considered, record:

- Scope of the statement and included routes/components.
- Engineering target, currently WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Test date, browsers, assistive technologies, devices and methods.
- Automated and manual test coverage.
- Known limitations and inaccessible content.
- Contact route for accessibility problems.
- Alternative-format request route.
- Reasonable adjustment and support approach.
- Review owner, review date and update process.

The statement must distinguish tested results from planned requirements and must
not say `WCAG 2.2 AA COMPLIANT` until appropriate rendered evidence exists.
