# Live Accessibility Verification Checklist

Future WordPress/staging execution checklist. No tests in this document have been
executed during WA-05F.

- Page title and document `lang` are correct.
- Landmarks, heading tree and one H1 are correct.
- Skip link appears on focus and reaches main content.
- Keyboard traversal works with no trap and logical focus order.
- Focus is visible and not obscured by sticky content.
- Mobile menu opens, closes and restores focus predictably.
- Text and non-text contrast pass with final rendered values.
- 200% text resize and 400% reflow do not clip essential content.
- Touch targets and spacing are usable in portrait and landscape.
- Images have correct informative/decorative alternatives.
- Complex diagrams have adjacent text equivalents.
- Link purpose and external destinations are clear.
- Reduced-motion preference is honoured.
- Embedded media, if authorised, has captions/transcripts and fallback.
- Forms, if authorised, have labels, instructions, errors and privacy explanation.
- Screen-reader reading order and status messages are meaningful.
- 404 and search, if present, provide accessible recovery.
- Legal, privacy and cookie controls are keyboard and screen-reader usable.
- Journal and 12 Pillar templates follow their accessible content patterns.
- Capture redacted evidence for every failure, fix and retest.
