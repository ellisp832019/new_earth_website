Status: APPROVED

## Core Decision

The current New Earth philosophical structure remains valid.

Preserve:

- Vision as direction
- Manifesto as principles
- Blueprint as the foundational framework
- all twelve existing Pillar names
- Inner Blueprint
- Transition Path
- New Earth in Practice
- local-first does not mean isolated
- technology serving human agency
- inquiry rather than prescription

The Blueprint must not become:

- the main learning library
- the project catalogue
- the R&D catalogue
- the product catalogue
- the complete ecosystem map
- an operating programme plan

## Blueprint Role

Blueprint is the foundational framework for understanding connected areas of life.

Its secondary role is to help people choose a meaningful starting point:

- a Pillar
- Inner Blueprint
- Transition Path
- New Earth in Practice

## Self-Sovereignty Language

Use the term selectively.

Prefer practical explanations around:

- knowledge
- capability
- informed choice
- meaningful control
- responsibility
- cooperation
- personal agency

Avoid:

- absolutist independence
- anti-government framing
- political ideology
- rejection of shared responsibility
- isolationist interpretations

## Human-Governed Technology

Preserve the principle that technology should:

- support human agency
- remain accountable
- make purpose and limitations understandable
- support informed choice
- use meaningful permissions
- permit review/questioning where appropriate
- avoid unnecessary dependency

These are principles and questions, not claims that every current New Earth
system already satisfies them.

## Local-First Principle

Preserve:

LOCAL-FIRST DOES NOT MEAN ISOLATED.

Support:

- capability close to affected people/places
- adaptation to local conditions
- shared knowledge
- cooperation
- wider coordination where useful

Do not imply all infrastructure/services/data must be local.
