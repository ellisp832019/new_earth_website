# Website V2 Content Production Sequence

WA-05 production proceeds in waves. Each wave drafts from the authority,
terminology, claims, and page inventory records. A page is not ready for
publication until its owner, evidence class, accessibility, legal, and
cross-link checks pass.

## Wave 1 - Core orientation

Pages: Vision, Manifesto, Blueprint, Ecosystem, About, Get Involved.
Inputs: approved strategy, blueprint sources, WA-02 routes, and existing page
masters. Outputs: plain-language orientation drafts and reviewed links.
Gate: New Earth voice, fact/vision separation, owner approval, and no internal
planning language. Blockers: conflicting doctrine, missing organisation facts,
or unsupported participation claims.

## Wave 2 - Pillar framework

Pages: Pillars hub and all 12 pillar pages.
Inputs: pillar sources, approved names/order, terminology register. Outputs:
consistent pillar drafts with definitions, relationships, and text alternatives.
Gate: source consolidation and safety/legal review where relevant. Blockers:
unresolved definitions, unsupported outcomes, or missing owner review.

## Wave 3 - Projects and technology

Pages: Projects, MicroGrow, MicroGrow AI Lab, Field Scanner, New Earth Living,
BioCalm, and approved public technology explanations.
Inputs: project records, asset controls, and technical evidence dependencies.
Outputs: status-labelled project drafts. Gate: project-owner evidence, technical
review, and explicit distinction between research, concept, prototype,
development, and availability. Blockers: missing evidence, readiness claims,
health/safety claims, or private technical details.

## Wave 4 - Learning and trust

Pages: Journal, Resources, FAQ and Glossary, Progress and Transparency, and
Research and Development.
Inputs: editorial sources, terminology register, and evidence records. Outputs:
useful public learning and trust content. Gate: editorial owner, claim review,
plain language, and legal review where needed. Blockers: unpublished claims,
unverified progress, or unclear ownership.

## Wave 5 - People, creator lanes and participation

Pages: Peter's creator lane, New Earth Practical Guides, Hayley's New Earth -
Conscious Living lane, community and collaboration pathways, and New Earth
Builders external pathway.
Inputs: creator approvals, partner evidence, and participation boundaries.
Outputs: attributed, consented, clearly bounded drafts. Gate: creator or partner
approval, privacy review, and relationship wording. Blockers: missing consent,
partnership ambiguity, or unsupported availability.

## Wave 6 - Legal and publication controls

Pages: privacy, cookies, terms, disclaimer, accessibility, and company
disclosures.
Inputs: tracked legal sources and verified company facts. Outputs: reviewed
publication-ready legal pages. Gate: professional legal review, consent/tooling
alignment, and final owner sign-off. Blockers: unresolved legal wording or
unverified company and service details.
