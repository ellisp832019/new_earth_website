# New Earth: Local First, Community First, Stewardship First

## Website narrative control

**New Earth starts where we are.**

People care for themselves, their homes, their communities and their piece of
Earth. New Earth favours local capability, stewardship and resilience before
unnecessary dependence on distant systems.

Local-first does not mean isolated. Strong local communities can connect
outward, share knowledge, cooperate, support one another and form wider
networks while retaining meaningful local agency.

Technology should strengthen this capability rather than centralise control
away from the people who depend on it.
